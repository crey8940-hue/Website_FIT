About us

One place for everything lost on campus.

Our background

Students carry phones, wallets, IDs and school materials all day. When something goes missing, the search usually means scrolling through chats and posts where reports get buried.

FINDIT brings it all into one organized platform for Davao del Norte State College, so lost items have a clear path back to their owners.

Our mission

Give the school community one simple, trusted platform to recover lost belongings and save everyone time.

Our vision

A campus where every lost item finds its way home.
